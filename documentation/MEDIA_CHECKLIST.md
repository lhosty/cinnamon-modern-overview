## Recommended video

Record at native resolution and 30 or 60 fps. Keep the clip under 25 seconds.

Suggested sequence:

1. Desktop → Super → Windows.
2. Navigate windows with arrow keys.
3. Super twice → Applications.
4. Type an application query.
5. Type `2+2` to show calculator and web results.
6. Return to Windows.
7. Swipe to another workspace.
8. Drag a window across workspaces.
9. Close with Super and show the panel restoring cleanly.

Avoid showing private documents, terminal history, email addresses, internal URLs or organization-specific information.
